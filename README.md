# Global & Regional Mental Health Disease Burden Analysis (DALYs)

## 🩺 Epidemiological Context & Public Health Significance
Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALYs) represent a crucial metric in global health, quantifying the gap between current health status and an ideal health situation where the entire population lives to an advanced age, free of disease and disability. This project analyzes the longitudinal trends of mental and substance use disorders as a share of the total disease burden, focusing on Vietnam and regional benchmarks.

## 🎯 Analytical Objectives
- To visualize and benchmark the macroeconomic health burden of mental disorders in Vietnam against global and regional countries (Thailand, Singapore) from 1990 to 2019.
- To construct a Predictive Linear Regression model forecasting the upcoming public health burden in Vietnam up to the years 2025 and 2030.

## 📊 Analytical Insights & Forecasting Results
- **Historical Trajectory:** Vietnam experienced a significant statistical surge in mental health burden, rising from **3.29%** of total DALYs in 1990 to **4.63%** in 2019.
- **Predictive Forecasting:** The predictive model projects a continuing upward linear trend, estimating that mental disorders will account for **5.08%** of total domestic disease burden by 2025 and reach **5.28%** by 2030.

## 🛡️ Preventive Health Policy Implications
1. **Resource Allocation:** University health infrastructure and government funding must scale proactively to meet the predicted ~14% expansion in mental health demands by 2030.
2. **Preventive Screening:** Implement preventative psychiatric and psychological early-screening frameworks to bend the curve of the projected DALY increase.
